About Jakkidi's Pharma

About the company

Established in 2018, Jakkidi's Pharma supplies pharmaceutical products, microbiology media, reagents, instruments and accessories to the pharmaceutical industry.

Alongside supply, the company acts as technical consultant to pharmaceutical manufacturers implementing the regulatory requirements that govern their microbiology and quality operations.
